Connect Stripe to start collecting payments

Follow these 4 steps to activate subscriptions — takes about 10 minutes.

1

Create a Stripe account

Go to stripe.com and sign up for free. Stripe takes 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction — no monthly fee to Stripe itself.

2

Create your subscription products

In the Stripe dashboard go to Products → Add Product. Create "InvoiceAI Pro" at $12/month and "InvoiceAI Agency" at $29/month with recurring billing.

3

Get your Payment Link URLs

In each product, click Payment Link → Create Link. Copy the URL — it looks like buy.stripe.com/abc123. Paste it into the buttons below.

4

Set up your webhook (optional)

In Developers → Webhooks add your site URL + /webhook to listen for payment_intent.succeeded and unlock Pro features automatically.

Common questions

How does the free trial work?

Start Pro or Agency free for 14 days — no credit card required. At the end of the trial, you'll be asked to add payment details to continue. Cancel anytime before with zero charge.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings at any moment. You keep access until the end of your billing period. No cancellation fees, no questions asked.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards via Stripe — Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and more. Stripe also supports Apple Pay and Google Pay in supported browsers.

Is my data safe?

Invoice data is stored locally in your browser by default. No invoice content is sent to our servers. Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe, which is PCI DSS Level 1 certified.
